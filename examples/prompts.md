# 自写输出用例

这些是本次按候选 Skill 编译的 recorded fixtures，不是独立模型路由或盲评。前三条用于真实 sd2.5 生成，其余验证输出边界。

## cinema-01

需求：夜班修表匠把修好的表递给离开的客人，听到熟悉的报时声停了一下。克制、自然的电影表演，不要煽情。

当前模式：创建 / 纯文本

中文 Prompt

```text
深夜修表铺里，一位穿灰色工作围裙的中年修表匠坐在木工作台后，把一只已修好的旧腕表轻推到台面右侧。画面右侧一只客人的手拿走腕表并退出画面。腕表离开时响起两声清脆报时。修表匠原本正收回的右手停住，抬眼看向画面右侧的门口，停顿一拍，再低头用拇指抚平桌上一小块起皱的擦表布。全程一个固定的平视中近景，工作台和他的两只手始终可见，最后停在他低头抚平布的姿态。左侧台灯发出温暖柔光，后方橱窗透入很弱的蓝灰夜光，肤质自然，木面有真实磨损。声音仅有店内轻微时钟滴答、腕表两声报时和衣料摩擦；没有对白，没有配乐，没有字幕。表演幅度小，不流泪，不突然微笑。
```

English Prompt

```text
In a watch repair shop late at night, a middle-aged watchmaker in a gray work apron sits behind a wooden bench and gently slides one repaired old wristwatch toward the right side of the counter. A customer's hand enters from screen right, takes the watch, and leaves the frame. As the watch leaves, it makes two clear chimes. The watchmaker's withdrawing right hand pauses; he looks toward the doorway off screen right, holds for one beat, then lowers his gaze and smooths a small wrinkled polishing cloth with his thumb. One locked eye-level medium close-up throughout, keeping the bench and both of his hands visible. End holding on his lowered gaze and hand smoothing the cloth. A desk lamp on the left casts soft warm light; faint blue-gray night light enters from the rear shop window. Natural skin texture and visibly worn wood. Sound consists only of quiet ticking clocks, the two watch chimes, and fabric movement. No dialogue, music, or subtitles. Keep the performance small, without tears or a sudden smile.
```

## animation-01

需求：纸艺商业动画：一张蓝纸折成帆船，向右滑过桌面，停住。纸张有厚度和折痕。

当前模式：创建 / 纯文本

中文 Prompt

```text
奶油白桌面中央只有一张钴蓝色方形纸。固定的三分之四俯视近景，全程不切镜。前两秒，纸张四角依次抬起并沿清楚的直线折痕折叠；接下来三秒，同一张纸连续折成一艘有三角船帆和窄船体的蓝色纸帆船，船帆竖起后轻轻回弹一次。随后纸船沿桌面向画面右侧滑行一个船身的距离，缓慢停稳；最后一秒保持完整纸船静止。纸艺定格动画质感，纸边有细小纤维与真实厚度，折痕压紧，折叠时保持同一张纸的蓝色与数量，不增加独立纸片。柔和的大面积光源从左上方照下，船底接触阴影始终贴着桌面。只保留细微折纸声和纸底摩擦桌面的声音，无人手，无文字，无音乐。
```

English Prompt

```text
Only one cobalt-blue square sheet of paper rests at the center of a cream-white tabletop. A locked close three-quarter overhead view, with no cuts. During the first two seconds, the four corners lift in sequence and fold along clear straight creases. Over the next three seconds, the same sheet continuously folds into a blue paper sailboat with a triangular sail and a narrow hull; the sail gently rebounds once as it rises. The boat then slides one hull-length toward screen right along the tabletop and gradually settles. Hold the completed boat still for the final second. Paper-craft stop-motion appearance, with tiny edge fibers, visible paper thickness, and compressed creases. Preserve the blue color and the single sheet throughout folding; do not add separate pieces. A broad soft light from upper left creates a contact shadow attached to the tabletop beneath the boat. Only subtle paper-folding sounds and paper rubbing against the table. No hands, text, or music.
```

## tvc-01

需求：创意TVC：一圈水变成无品牌香水瓶，瓶子成形后不再变形，留出落幅。

当前模式：创建 / 纯文本

中文 Prompt

```text
深灰色石台上方悬着一圈透明的水，水环竖立，正对镜头。前两秒，水环缓慢向中心收拢，底部先接触石台；接下来三秒，水由下向上凝结成一只透明的方形玻璃香水瓶，瓶内保留少量淡琥珀色液体，一枚纯黑圆柱瓶盖在顶端凝结完成。第5秒以后瓶子已经完全成形，形状与瓶盖不再变化，水滴沿外壁向下滑落。最后三秒，镜头缓慢推进到完整瓶身的产品近景，保持瓶盖、瓶底都在画面内。只有一个瓶子，无品牌，无标签，无文字。背景为安静的深灰渐变；左侧长条柔光在玻璃边缘形成细亮线，右侧低亮反光保留方形轮廓，石台能看到瓶底接触阴影。声音从轻柔水流过渡到一声轻玻璃鸣响，结尾安静，无旁白，无音乐。
```

English Prompt

```text
A single transparent ring of water hangs above a dark-gray stone plinth, upright and facing the camera. During the first two seconds, the ring slowly gathers toward its center, with its bottom touching the stone first. Over the next three seconds, the water solidifies from bottom to top into one clear square glass perfume bottle containing a small amount of pale amber liquid; one plain black cylindrical cap finishes forming at the top. After second five the bottle is fully formed: its geometry and cap remain unchanged while droplets slide down its outer walls. During the final three seconds, the camera slowly pushes into a product close-up that keeps both cap and base within frame. Exactly one bottle, without branding, labels, or text. Quiet dark-gray gradient background. A long soft light on the left draws a narrow highlight along the glass; a dim reflection on the right preserves its square silhouette. Keep the bottle's contact shadow visible on the stone. Sound moves from gentle flowing water to one soft glass chime, then settles into quiet. No narration or music.
```
